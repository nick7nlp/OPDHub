## 📎 Papers Citing Our Survey (2604.00626)

### 2605.12483 — Beyond GRPO and OPD: Sparse-to-Dense Reward Principle
**Authors**: Hejian Sang, Yuanda Xu, Zhengze Zhou, Ran He, Zhipeng Wang, Alborz Geramifard
**Core thesis**: GRPO (sparse RL) and OPD (dense teacher supervision) are different reward-density regimes. The optimal allocation: use sparse reward upstream on teacher for discovery, dense OPD bridge to compress into student, then optional student-side sparse RL.
**Key results**:
- GRPO→8B teacher RL, then OPD→1.7B student: 79.3% MATH (vs 75.9% direct GRPO)
- Forward-KL warmup + OPD consistently strongest pre-RL
- Pattern replicates on Llama with 70B teacher
**Survey relevance**: Validates OPD as essential "bridge" step in post-training pipeline. Complements our Section 3 (Training Pipeline).

### 2605.11739 — Learning to Foresee: EffOPD
**Authors**: Yuchen Cai, Ding Cao, ..., Junfeng Fang (USTC/Tencent/NUS)
**Core thesis**: OPD's efficiency stems from "foresight" — establishing stable update trajectory early.
- **Module-Allocation Level**: OPD identifies low-utility modules early and suppresses their updates
- **Update-Direction Level**: OPD has stronger low-rank concentration, dominant subspaces align with final update subspace early
**Method**: EffOPD — plug-and-play 3x acceleration via adaptive extrapolation along current direction
**Survey relevance**: Provides parameter-dynamics perspective for our Section on "Why OPD Works". Same research group as SOD.

### 2605.08741 — OPHSD: On-Policy Harness Self-Distillation
**Authors**: Zhengyang Zhao, Lu Ma, Wentao Zhang (PKU)
**Core thesis**: Inference-time harnesses (draft-verify, plan-solve) can be used as temporary teacher scaffolds for self-distillation.
- Harness-augmented model serves as teacher; student learns to internalize the harness behavior
- After training, reattaching harness provides NO additional benefit → harness fully internalized
**Key results**: +10.83% over OPSD on HMMT25
**Survey relevance**: Novel paradigm — harness as temporary training scaffold for OPD. Fits our Section on Self-Distillation variants.

### 2605.07725 — SOD: Step-wise On-policy Distillation
**Authors**: Qiyong Zhong, Mao Zheng, Mingyang Song, ... Junfeng Fang (ZJU/Tencent/USTC/NUS)
**NOTE**: nickmysong is co-author!
**Core thesis**: Standard OPD fails for tool-integrated reasoning (TIR) because erroneous tool calls cascade, amplifying student-teacher divergence.
- SOD: adaptively reweights distillation per step based on step-level divergence
- Attenuates misleading teacher signals in high-divergence regions
**Key results**: Up to 20.86% improvement. 0.6B student gets 26.13% average@32 on AIME 2025.
**Survey relevance**: Extends OPD to agentic/TIR domain. Directly fits our Section on OPD Applications.

### 2605.07711 — SimCT: Cross-Tokenizer OPD
**Core thesis**: Standard OPD requires shared tokenizer. SimCT enables cross-tokenizer OPD via vocabulary mapping and similarity-based token alignment.
**Survey relevance**: Addresses a practical limitation we discuss — tokenizer compatibility in OPD.

### 2605.07396 — ROPD: Rubric-based On-Policy Distillation
**Core thesis**: Replace logit-based OPD with rubric-based signals. A Rubricator contrasts teacher/student rollouts to synthesize rubrics, a Verifier scores student rollouts.
- Enables black-box teacher (no logit access needed — works with GPT-5.2!)
- 10x sample efficiency over logit-based OPD
- Cross-architecture distillation without tokenizer alignment
**Key results**: Competitive with or surpasses logit-based OPD despite never accessing teacher logits
**Survey relevance**: Opens new paradigm — rubric as alternative to logit signals. Major addition to our taxonomy.

### 2605.06597 — UniSD: Unified Self-Distillation Framework
**Authors**: Yiqiao Jin, ... Josiah Hester, Jindong Wang, Srijan Kumar (Georgia Tech / W&M)
**Core thesis**: Systematic study of self-distillation. Integrates multi-teacher agreement, EMA teacher, contrastive learning, feature matching, divergence clipping.
- UniSD* (combined pipeline): +5.4 over base, +2.8 over strongest baseline across 6 benchmarks/6 models
**Survey relevance**: Provides systematic ablation of SD components — complements our self-distillation taxonomy.

## 🔬 OPD Analysis Papers (not citing us but highly relevant)

### 2605.11182 — The Many Faces of OPD: Pitfalls, Mechanisms, and Fixes
**Authors**: Siqi Zhu et al. (UIUC/RUC/PKU)
**Three failure mechanisms identified**:
1. **Distribution mismatch**: Teacher conditioned on student prefixes that diverge from teacher's reasoning
2. **Biased TopK reverse-KL gradients**: Optimization instability from restricted vocabulary KL
3. **OPSD PI aggregation failure**: When privileged information is instance-specific, student learns PI-free policy that averages over all PI-conditioned teachers → insufficient
**Fixes**: Stop-gradient TopK, RLVR-adapted teachers, SFT-stabilized students
**Survey relevance**: Directly addresses failure modes we should catalog in survey.

### 2605.10889 — Unmasking OPD (Apple)
**Training-free diagnostic framework**:
- Derives ideal per-node gradient (parameter update that maximally increases success probability)
- Gradient alignment score = cosine similarity between ideal gradient and distillation gradient
- Key finding: Distillation guidance has higher alignment on INCORRECT rollouts than correct ones
- No single universally effective configuration emerges
**Survey relevance**: Important diagnostic tool for understanding when OPD helps.

### 2605.09253 — Rock Tokens: Stumbling Blocks in OPD
**Phenomenon**: Even after training saturation, ~6% of vocabulary types (~18% of token frequency) maintain persistently high loss.
**Rock Tokens are**: Syntactic scaffolding — formatting delimiters, whitespace, discourse markers ("So", "Wait")
**Two paradoxes**:
1. Despite providing disproportionate gradient norms, Rock Tokens themselves remain stagnant
2. Causal intervention shows they contribute negligibly to reasoning performance
**Implication**: Strategic gradient sparsification (bypassing Rock Tokens) maintains performance with less compute
**Survey relevance**: Token-level analysis of OPD dynamics — complements our mechanism analysis section.

## 🧪 Self-Distillation Methods

### 2605.12400 — OGLS-SD: Outcome-Guided Logit Steering
**Problem**: OPSD collapse from teacher-student distributional mismatch (even from same model)
- Two sources: (1) reflection-induced bias in self-teacher, (2) response template mismatch
**Method**: Uses verifiable outcome rewards to contrast successful/failed trajectories, calibrates teacher logits
**Survey relevance**: Addresses OPSD stability — important for self-distillation section.

### 2605.11613 — CREDIT: Contrastive Reward from Distillation (Xiaohongshu)
**Analysis**: Self-distillation token reward = Bayesian filtering increment; trajectory sum = pMI between response and feedback
- pMI can be raised by input-specific reasoning OR input-generic shortcuts
**Method**: Contrastive baseline isolates input-specific component with batch contrastive
**Survey relevance**: Theoretical grounding for self-distillation rewards.

### 2605.11609 — AntiSD: Anti-Self-Distillation (Xiaohongshu)
**Key insight**: Standard OPSD inflates teacher confidence on "structural" tokens (connectives, verifiable claims) while deflating "deliberation" tokens (Wait, Let, Maybe)
**Method**: ASCEND divergence instead of descending it — reverses per-token sign. Entropy-triggered gate disables when teacher entropy collapses.
**Results**: GRPO-level accuracy in 2-10x fewer steps, +11.5pt improvement
**Survey relevance**: Challenges the fundamental direction of self-distillation — important conceptual contribution.

### 2605.11458 — ATESD: Adaptive Teacher Exposure (ByteDance)
**Problem**: Full-exposure teacher (sees complete reference) creates too-strong targets when student is weak
**Method**: Beta-policy controller determines how much reference to reveal to teacher. Discounted learning-progress reward for delayed credit assignment.
**Results**: +0.95/+2.05/+2.33 Average@12 over OPSD for Qwen3-{1.7B,4B,8B}
**Survey relevance**: New axis for self-distillation optimization.

### 2605.09548 — COPSD: Crosslingual On-Policy Self-Distillation (LMU Munich)
**Method**: Same model as student/teacher. Student sees low-resource problem; teacher gets problem translation + reference in English.
**Evaluation**: 17 low-resource African languages
**Results**: Consistently outperforms GRPO; improves format adherence and test-time scaling
**Survey relevance**: First multilingual application of OPSD.

## ⚡ Efficiency & Optimization Methods

### 2605.07804 — Prune-OPD: Efficient OPD for Long-Horizon Reasoning (HKUST-GZ)
**Problem**: In long-horizon OPD, student prefix diverges from teacher's thought process → teacher reward loses local exploitability
**Method**: Monitors student-teacher compatibility (top-k overlap) in real-time. Detects prefix-drift → monotonically down-weights subsequent rewards + triggers truncation.
**Results**: 37.6-68.0% training time reduction while maintaining/improving AIME/HMMT performance
**Survey relevance**: Practical efficiency improvement for long-context OPD.

### 2605.07865 — vOPD: OPD with Control Variate Baseline (SNU)
**Problem**: OPD's single-sample Monte Carlo estimator has high gradient variance
**Method**: Casts OPD as policy-gradient RL. Value function = per-token negative reverse KL (available from forward pass, no additional critic). Top-k approximation further lowers cost.
**Results**: Matches expensive full-vocabulary baseline at lower cost
**Survey relevance**: Principled RL-theoretic stabilization of OPD training.

### 2605.08873 — CoDistill-GRPO (Google)
**Problem**: GRPO fails on small models (sparse rewards)
**Method**: Bidirectional co-distillation: small model uses on-policy KD reward from large; large model trains on small's rollouts with importance reweighting (18% speedup for large)
**Results**: +11.6pp over base, +6.0pp over GRPO for Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B
**Survey relevance**: Novel co-distillation paradigm for OPD.

### 2605.08776 — MPD: Mixed-Policy Distillation
**Method**: Given student trajectory, teacher REWRITES it into concise version. Student trains via KL on compressed trajectory. "Mixed-policy": structure from student, content from teacher.
**Results**: 27.1% token reduction while improving accuracy (Qwen3-1.7B)
**Survey relevance**: Reasoning compression through OPD — new application direction.

## 🔧 Other OPD Methods

### 2605.12227 — dGRPO: GRPO + OPD for Long-Context (IST Lisbon)
**Method**: Augments GRPO with dense OPD guidance from teacher. Introduces LONGBLOCKS benchmark.
**Survey relevance**: Long-context recipe combining RL + OPD.

### 2605.08737 — ListOPD: Extrapolation Cliff for Structured Outputs (NTU Singapore)
**Key insight**: Reward extrapolation coefficient λ > λ* causes format collapse in structured JSON outputs.
- Derives closed-form clip-safety threshold
- Operating below λ*, 1.7B student reaches 8B-SFT parity
**Survey relevance**: First formal analysis of OPD stability boundary for structured outputs.

### 2605.06387 — AOPD: Asymmetric On-Policy Distillation (Meituan)
**Three weaknesses of standard OPD advantage-weighted gradient**: (1) high variance in negative regions, (2) vanishing gradients at zero advantage, (3) exploration bottlenecks
**Method**: Exploitation on positive-advantage tokens + localized teacher divergence minimization on non-positive tokens
**Results**: +4.09/+8.34 under strong/weak initialization
**Survey relevance**: Improves OPD's exploration-exploitation balance.

### 2605.05940 — NPD: Near-Policy Distillation (Huawei)
**Method**: Async generation (decouple from training) → SFT with sequence packing. ∆-IFD filtering to prevent off-policy drift.
**Results**: 8.1x speedup, state-of-the-art 68.73% for openPangu-Embedded-1B
**Survey relevance**: Major practical speedup for OPD deployment.

### 2605.09725 — BRTS: Best-of-N Teacher Rollout Selection (JHU/TikTok)
**Problem**: Standard OPD uses single stochastic teacher rollout — can be incorrect/uninformative
**Method**: Generate N teacher rollouts, select best one (by correctness + quality)
**Survey relevance**: Simple but effective OPD improvement.

### 2605.07505 — LiteGUI: OPD for GUI Agents (Moore Threads)
**First application of OPD to GUI agent domain**: Guided-OPD with oracle trajectories + dynamic retrieval from multi-solution pool + Multi-solution Dual-level GRPO
**Survey relevance**: OPD in new domain (GUI agents).
