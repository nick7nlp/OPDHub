Dear Qin et al.,

Thank you for the note. You are correct on the chronology. We have verified the arXiv submission dates: MSD (2605.02971) was submitted on May 3, 2026, and COPSD (2605.09548) on May 10, 2026, so MSD precedes COPSD by seven days. The "MSD extends COPSD" framing in §5.3.1 was inaccurate, and we apologize for the mischaracterization.

We have revised the two paragraphs in §5.3.1 to describe the two works as parallel and contemporaneous, following your suggested wording. The relevant passage now reads:

> "MSD~\citep{2605.02971} and COPSD~\citep{2605.09548} independently explore cross-lingual privileged-information-based self-distillation in different domains, with MSD focusing on multilingual safety alignment and COPSD on cross-lingual mathematical reasoning."

The bridging sentence at the end of the "Cross-lingual reasoning via PI" paragraph has also been updated to refer to MSD as a contemporaneous and independent work rather than a downstream extension.

Other references to MSD in the manuscript (the §5 mindmap, Tables 4 and 6, and the §future safety paragraph) describe method properties only and do not imply a chronology, so we have left them as-is.

The corrections will appear in the next arXiv update of the survey.
